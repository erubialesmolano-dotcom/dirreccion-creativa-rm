# Sourcing sound effects, ambiences and music for commercial short videos (late 2026)

Research date: 24 Nov 2026. Many vendor pricing pages render prices in JavaScript, so several figures come from third-party aggregators (flagged). Verify every price at checkout. Prices are USD unless stated, VAT excluded.

## 1. Music subscription libraries: prices, client work, paid ads, use after cancelling, Content ID, stems, search

### Takeaway
For an agency that delivers to clients and runs paid ads, the cheap creator tiers are not enough on any of the big libraries. Client work and ads need the "Pro"/"Business" tier: Epidemic Pro or Business, Artlist Pro licence (Music Pro, Music & SFX Pro or Max), Soundstripe Pro and up, Uppbeat Pro or Business. These cost roughly $15-$75 a month. The typical rule after cancelling: videos published while subscribed stay cleared, and nothing new can be made. Epidemic Business ($30/mo billed annually, $75 billed monthly) is the most explicit fit for "client work + paid ads + publish on the client's channels."

### Cited Findings
**Epidemic Sound**
- Business plan: $30/month billed annually, or $75 billed monthly. The page says you can "Produce client work and publish on your channels or your client's", and lists "paid ads" and "influencer campaigns." Each admin can safelist one channel per marketing platform. "Once a project is published, the license stays with it." Includes music, SFX and AI voiceovers. Says "No PRO claims" and "No late-stage takedowns from social platforms" — [Epidemic Business plan](https://www.epidemicsound.com/our-plans/business-plan/)
- Business Plus: from $100/mo billed annually. Up to 10 channels and 10 seats, plus apps/games/OTT and extended rights for local broadcast and live events — [Epidemic Business plan](https://www.epidemicsound.com/our-plans/business-plan/); [CreatorStackClub](https://www.creatorstackclub.com/software/epidemic-sound)
- Pro plan: includes freelancer client work with "full sublicensing rights" for clients' channels, and digital ads for online campaigns ("not available to major corporations, publishers, or government entities"). 3 channels per platform, stems, 250,000 SFX, AI voiceovers (250,000 credits/mo, 3 voice replicas). Videos published during an active subscription are "cleared forever." Videos published after it ends are not — [Epidemic Pro plan](https://www.epidemicsound.com/our-plans/pro-plan/)

**Free options**

### Inferences
- Recommended stack for Erik: **Epidemic Business** (or Pro if he's a freelancer below the revenue thresholds) as the main music+SFX library, since its official page covers client channels and paid ads. Alternatively **Artlist Music & SFX Pro** (~$299/yr, annual upfront), which also feeds AI tools. Add Musicbed or Marmoset per project only for hero brand spots needing premium or bespoke-sounding tracks.
- Keep a licence ledger per client video (track ID, licence/plan, date, account, channel safelisted). Several services tie protection to safelisted channels and active-subscription dates.
- Safelisting usually covers YouTube Content ID. For client channels on Instagram/TikTok, the licence certificate matters more than safelisting.

### Gaps
- Exact Epidemic Creator/Pro monthly prices and EUR pricing weren't verifiable (they render in JS). Aggregator figures conflict.
- Musicvine, Marmoset, Musicbed, Envato Elements and PremiumBeat 2026 prices: not verified. Background knowledge, unverified: Envato Elements ~€16.50/mo annual for individuals; Musicbed and Marmoset per-track licences often $50-$500+ depending on use and paid media.
- Pixabay music licence and Content ID behaviour: the FAQ fetch failed. Background knowledge, unverified: free commercial use, no attribution, but known for false Content ID claims because some uploaders register tracks. Low priority for client ads.
- Search features (BPM / "similar to" / reference-track upload) weren't confirmed per vendor, except Soundstripe's Supe AI. Background, unverified: Epidemic and Artlist offer BPM/mood/energy filters and "similar tracks"; Musicbed and Soundstripe support reference-based search.

## 2. SFX libraries (incl. UI and motion-graphics packs)

### Takeaway
Most big music subscriptions now bundle large SFX catalogues: Epidemic 250k, Soundstripe 115k+, Artlist on Music & SFX plans. For a short-video agency that may cover whooshes, risers, pops and clicks. Add Soundly (cloud library + local library manager) and the free Sonniss GDC bundles for depth.

### Cited Findings
- Epidemic Pro catalogue includes 250,000 SFX — [Epidemic Pro](https://www.epidemicsound.com/our-plans/pro-plan/)
- Soundstripe lists 115,000+ SFX — [Soundstripe pricing](https://www.soundstripe.com/library/pricing)
- Soundly tiers: Free (3,000+ SFX cloud library, index up to 10,000 local files), 24hr Pass, Pro Monthly (full Soundly Pro cloud library, unlimited local indexing, 10GB collaboration space), Multi-user (upload your own libraries to the cloud, priced by storage). No prices on the FAQ page — [Soundly FAQ](https://getsoundly.com/faq/what-are-the-differences-between-the-subscriptions/)
- Sonniss GDC 2026 bundle: 347+ WAV files, 7.47 GB (voices, vehicles, ambiences, foley). Royalty-free and commercially usable, "no strings attached," lifetime licence. Download at https://gdc.sonniss.com/ (past years' bundles also there) — [Bedroom Producers Blog](https://bedroomproducersblog.com/2026/03/16/sonniss-gdc-2026-bundle/)
- Sonniss also ran a free SFX giveaway in August 2026 — [BPB Aug 2026](https://bedroomproducersblog.com/2026/08/20/sonniss-free-sfx-august/) (not fetched)
- Audiio bundles a large SFX library with music — [Soundstripe blog](https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/musicbed-alternatives)

### Inferences
- UI/motion packs (whoosh, riser, pop, click, glitch, swipe, typing) are cheaper to get from the bundled subscriptions or one-off packs (A Sound Effect, BOOM) than from a separate SFX subscription.

### Gaps
None of these were researched in this pass (time limit). Background knowledge, all unverified:
- **freesound.org:** each sound carries its own licence (CC0, CC-BY, CC-BY-NC). CC-BY needs attribution, which is awkward in ads. CC-BY-NC is **not** usable for client or commercial work.
- **Zapsplat:** free with attribution, or paid Gold without attribution.
- **Soundsnap:** subscription with unlimited downloads.
- **BOOM Library and Pro Sound Effects:** one-off pro packs, perpetual licence.
- **A Sound Effect:** marketplace with many UCS-tagged indie packs.
- **Splice:** samples are royalty-free for music. Using them as SFX in video is generally allowed, but check the terms.
- **Motion Array:** subscription with SFX and UI packs.
- **Sound Ideas:** legacy broadcast libraries, upgraded UCS data since summer 2025 — [Sound Ideas UCS page](https://portal.sound-ideas.com/Page/ucs-data-upgrade) (not fetched).

## 3. AI audio (ElevenLabs, Suno, Udio, Stable Audio, Adobe Firefly, Lyria, Higgsfield): terms and legal risk

### Takeaway
ElevenLabs (paid plans) and Adobe Firefly are the cleanest AI options for client ads. ElevenLabs Music allows all online/offline commercial use except film/TV/radio/studio games, though its Free-to-Pro tiers are "individual use only." Firefly is trained on licensed and public-domain content, with indemnity on enterprise plans. Suno v6 (Sept 2026) is partly licensed (WMG, BMG, Believe) and paid users get commercial rights, but UMG/Sony litigation is ongoing and the outputs may not be copyrightable. That makes it higher-risk for client ads. Udio can't export at all (walled garden), so it's unusable for delivery.

### Cited Findings
**ElevenLabs**
- Eleven Music: every plan except Enterprise Music allows "All online and offline commercial use permitted, except film, TV, radio, and Studio Games." Free, Starter, Creator and Pro are "For Individual Use Only." Scale is for entities with <10 employees, Business <50. Free requires attribution ("Eleven Music"); paid plans need none — [ElevenLabs Music Terms](https://elevenlabs.io/music-terms)
- Streaming distribution is prohibited on Free/Starter and allowed on Creator and up. Generation/download minutes per month: Starter 17/30, Creator 62/250, Pro 304/500, Scale 1,100/1,500, Business 4,800/4,000 — [DocumentaryTube](https://www.documentarytube.com/blog/elevenlabs-ai-music-generator-is-commercially-cleared-but-not-for-every-use/)
- General output rules: the free plan "cannot be used for any commercial purpose." Paid-plan content generated during the subscription can be used commercially "indefinitely." Beta Services outputs can't be used commercially — [ElevenLabs help](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/legal/can-i-publish-the-content-i-generate-on-the-platform)
- ElevenLabs runs a Music Marketplace — [ElevenLabs docs](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/music/marketplace) (not fetched)

**Suno**
- Warner settled and partnered in Nov 2025. UMG and Sony were still litigating as of late April 2026, and settlement talks reportedly stalled on 9 April 2026 — [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ai-music-news-april-2026)
- v6 launched 9 Sept 2026, built with WMG, BMG and Believe data, excluding Universal and Sony. Models: v6 and v6-wild (Pro/Premier) and v6-mini (free). Terms effective 3 Sept give Pro/Premier subscribers Suno's rights in the output, "subject to commercial-use restrictions," with no guarantee of copyright. Rightsholders may collect revenue from outputs on third-party platforms. Watermarking and fingerprinting are planned. UMG/Sony amended their complaint on 25 Aug with a YouTube stream-ripping claim, and Suno admitted using YT-DLP — [MBW](https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/suno-v6-ai-music-models-launch-in-partnership-with-wmg-bmg-and-believe)
- Downloads from 3 Sept 2026: Free 0, Pro 20/month, Premier 60/month (unlimited in Suno Studio for Premier). Stems count as one download. Paid downloads carry commercial rights. After cancelling, downloads drop to zero — [Suno Help FAQ](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13614785)

**Udio**
- Settled with UMG (Oct 2025). Licensing deals with UMG, WMG, Merlin and Kobalt. "Walled garden": generated music can't be downloaded or distributed off-platform — [MBW](https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/suno-v6-ai-music-models-launch-in-partnership-with-wmg-bmg-and-believe); [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ai-music-news-april-2026)

**Adobe Firefly**
- Generate Music, Generate Speech and Generate Sound Effects reached general availability on 20 Aug 2026. Generate Music reads the video and returns 4 options cut to length. Sound effects can be made from text or from a vocal imitation, synced to the action. Models: Firefly Music / Speech (ElevenLabs Multilingual v2 also available) / Audio. Output is described as "universally licensed," trained on licensed Adobe Stock and public-domain content. IP indemnification is on qualifying enterprise plans only. Credits per audio generation are unpublished. AU pricing: Standard A$16.49/mo (2,000 credits), Pro A$32.98 (4,000), Pro Plus A$55.99 promo — [iTWire](https://itwire.com/business-it-news/business-software/adobe-makes-firefly-a-full-audio-studio-and-the-licence-is-the-actual-product); [Adobe blog](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2026/08/20/adobe-firefly-expands-its-creative-ai-studio-generate-music-speech-and-sound-effects-in-one-place)

**Artlist AI**
- Artlist AI plans: Starter $11.99/mo yearly (16.5k credits), Core $23.99 (40k), Creator $41.67 (80k), including voiceover hours — [CCHound](https://www.cchound.com/artlist/artlist-subscription-plans-and-pricing/)
- Standard Artlist music licences don't automatically extend to AI-generated stems, and AI generation needs Max — [Activepieces Artlist](https://www.activepieces.com/blog/artlist-pricing-2026-what-every-plan-costs.md)

### Inferences
- For client ads: **ElevenLabs SFX/music on a paid plan, Firefly, or licensed library music**. Don't use free-tier outputs.
- An agency working for clients on an ElevenLabs "individual use only" plan may fall outside the music terms. Scale (<10 employees) or Business is the safer fit. Confirm with ElevenLabs.
- Suno: OK for own-brand experiments. For client paid ads, add a contract clause and a fallback, because of the copyrightability caveat and the open litigation.
- Save the prompt, date, plan and generation ID for every AI asset, as provenance.

- The ElevenLabs Sound Effects-specific licence wasn't fetched. The general paid-plan commercial rule applies.
- Not researched: Stable Audio (Stability AI) terms, Google Lyria / MusicFX commercial availability, Higgsfield audio licence. Higgsfield has sound-effect search/generation tools exposed via MCP, but its licence wasn't checked.

### Gaps

## 5. Platform music rules (Instagram/Meta, TikTok, YouTube) for business accounts and ads

### Takeaway
Business accounts and ads on TikTok must use the Commercial Music Library (CML), and CML tracks are cleared only inside TikTok. On Instagram, business accounts are limited to licensed/commercial audio such as Meta Sound Collection. Trending mainstream songs aren't usable for brands. For cross-posting the same edit to IG, TikTok and YouTube, use a library licence (Epidemic/Artlist etc.) rather than in-app sounds.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok CML: "pre-cleared global music library of 1 million songs," free for businesses. Covers organic content, video ads and branded content. Businesses "cannot use the general music library for commercial usage" — [TikTok Ads Help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article?aid=10005905)
- CML tracks "can only be shared within TikTok." The standard Sound Library in sponsored content is a "licensing violation." 60-second music cap. Neither library is allowed during TikTok Live — [Thematic](https://hellothematic.com/music-rules-tiktok-instagram-youtube/)
- Instagram business accounts are restricted from the full trending library. Meta Sound Collection has 14,000+ songs and sounds "cleared for commercial use in Reels, Stories, and ads." Mainstream music risks muting, blocking or reduced reach — [Thematic](https://hellothematic.com/music-rules-tiktok-instagram-youtube/)
- YouTube: Shorts of 1-3 minutes with an active Content ID claim get blocked — [Thematic](https://hellothematic.com/music-rules-tiktok-instagram-youtube/)
- Legal bulletin (Pérez-Llorca, 6 Mar 2026): not all Instagram/TikTok music can be used by brands. Rightsholders and collecting societies have "intensificado la vigilancia," with payment demands for unauthorised uses — [Pérez-Llorca](https://www.perezllorca.com/es-co/actualidad/boletin/la-musica-en-redes-sociales-lo-facil-sale-costoso/)

### Inferences
- Remotion workflow: always bake licensed library or AI music into the render. Don't rely on in-app sounds for client content. Personal-brand organic posts on creator accounts are a different case.

### Gaps
- Meta's own Sound Collection terms page wasn't fetched. The 14,000 figure comes from a third party.

## 6. Organizing a personal SFX library (UCS, metadata tools) and making it AI-agent-usable

### Takeaway
Use the free Universal Category System (UCS): filenames follow `CatID_FXName_CreatorID_SourceID.wav`, with 1,600+ category terms. Soundly (free tier included) and BaseHead can rename and embed BWF/iXML metadata. For an AI agent, a UCS-named folder tree plus a sidecar CSV/JSON index with loudness-normalised files makes the library searchable and scriptable (e.g. from Remotion).

### Cited Findings
- UCS pattern `CatID_FXName_CreatorID_SourceID.wav`: CatID like AMB or FOLEY, FXName short description, CreatorID the recordist or vendor, SourceID the library or project. Optional UserCategory/VendorCategory/UserData fields. "over 1,600+ category terms" from AIR to WOOD. Spreadsheet and tools at https://universalcategorysystem.com/. Compatible tools: Soundminer, Sound Particles Explorer, BaseHead, ReNamer, UCSRenamer — [Spencer Bruce](https://www.spencerbruce.com/blog1/2025/10/3/understanding-the-universal-category-system-ucs)
- UCS was originated as a standard used at Skywalker Sound and other studios — [Synthtopia](https://www.synthtopia.com/content/2023/02/08/the-universal-category-system-is-skywalker-sounds-standard-for-organizing-huge-libraries-of-sounds/) (title only)
- Soundly converts files and libraries to UCS (including renaming) and embeds BWF and iXML metadata. Its free version indexes local files and supports metadata and UCS conversion. The limit is 2,500 in that article vs 10,000 on the current FAQ — [Soundly news](https://getsoundly.com/news/create-ucs-compliant-files-with-metadata-in-soundly); [Soundly FAQ](https://getsoundly.com/faq/what-are-the-differences-between-the-subscriptions/)
- BaseHead publishes a UCS categories knowledge-base page — [BaseHead UCS](https://baseheadinc.com/kb/ucs-categories/)
- Academic work (DAFx 2026) on unifying SFX datasets with UCS — [DAFx26](https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/2026/papers/DAFx26_paper_51.pdf) (not fetched)

### Inferences (suggested agent-friendly structure)
- Folder tree: `SFX/<UCS Category>/<SubCategory>/` (e.g. `SFX/WHOOSH/SWISH/`, `SFX/UI/CLICK/`, `SFX/AMBIENCE/URBAN/`), plus `MUSIC/<source>/<mood>/` and `_LICENSES/`.
- Filenames: `WHSH_Fast Air Swish Short_ERK_Epidemic.wav`, i.e. UCS CatID first.
- Sidecar `index.csv`/`index.jsonl` with: path, ucs_catid, description, tags/moods, duration_ms, bpm/key (music), loudness LUFS, peak dBTP, source, licence type, licence URL/ID, allowed uses (client/ads), date acquired, attribution text.
- Normalisation (background knowledge, unverified):
  - Keep originals untouched.
  - Export "ready" copies as 48 kHz WAV with short-SFX peaks around -1 dBTP, or LUFS targets for beds/ambiences (social deliverables are usually mastered around -14 LUFS integrated). Use ffmpeg `loudnorm` or a batch tool.
  - Trim silence so the agent can align the waveform start to a frame in Remotion.
- Have the agent read the index rather than scan audio. Embedding-based search (e.g. CLAP) over descriptions is optional.

### Gaps
- Prices for Soundly Pro, BaseHead and Soundminer weren't retrieved.

## 7. Spain/EU specifics (SGAE etc.)

### Takeaway
Royalty-free/PRO-free libraries avoid collecting-society claims. Commercial use of repertoire music in Spain needs both sync and master rights (publishers/labels), and collecting societies actively pursue unlicensed brand use. No primary Spanish-specific source was gathered.

### Cited Findings
- Epidemic advertises "No PRO claims" on Business — [Epidemic Business](https://www.epidemicsound.com/our-plans/business-plan/); Spanish-language business licensing page: https://www.epidemicsound.com/es/how-it-works/business-music-licensing/
- Brands face payment demands from rightsholders and collecting societies for unlicensed social-media music — [Pérez-Llorca](https://www.perezllorca.com/es-co/actualidad/boletin/la-musica-en-redes-sociales-lo-facil-sale-costoso/) (Colombia edition; jurisdiction not Spain-specific)

### Gaps
- Not verified: SGAE (authors), AIE (performers) and AGEDI (producers) roles in advertising sync in Spain, and whether library tracks by SGAE-member composers can trigger claims. Recommend checking each library's "PRO-free" status for Spanish clients.
- Not researched: the EU AI Act transparency/labelling obligations for AI-generated audio in ads.
